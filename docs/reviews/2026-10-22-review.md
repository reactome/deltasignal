## Candidates for accuracy work

These come out of the review rather than out of the error census.

- **I1, recycle pool supply (#2).** A recycle pool should read the supply of the protein it
  recycles: its starting copies. Today the pool reads the mean of its complexes' producers,
  partners included, so a p107 or p130 knockout leaves two thirds of the pool, and their
  overexpression is diluted. Mitotic G1 is a tuning pathway with many RBL1/RBL2 cases.
- **I2, base weights (#3).** The resting share of a pool's non-base form should not depend on
  how many variant copies the generator emits. Splitting the base weight across the k copies
  would restore φ₀, and with it the 10× ceiling on β-catenin folds (today 181×, so APC and
  AMER1 knockouts rail at 100×).
- **I3, `no_path` on the solved network (#31).** Recompute the specs/079 census on the network
  the solver runs. The cases that move from "no route" to "routed" are the ones a solver rule
  can still reach, and they size I1/I2-type work honestly.
